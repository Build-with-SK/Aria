## WHAT IS BEING ASKED, AND WHERE THE LINE IS

The owner has access to a powerful university machine and wants ARIA to have
her own model — one that evolves, reads, writes, creates, sees, senses, and
reasons at a very high level.

Most of that is achievable. One part of it is not, and the next session must
say so on day one rather than discovering it in week three.

### The ladder, with the line drawn on it

| Tier | What it is | Cost | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Serve a large open-weights model** | Llama-3.3-70B or Qwen2.5-72B at Q4-Q8. Frontier-adjacent quality. | ~40-80 GB VRAM. One node. | **Do this first.** Transforms her capability immediately. |
| **2. Fine-tune it into HER model** | QLoRA on her own resolved predictions, the owner's vault, and financial texts. Weights that exist nowhere else. | 2-4 GPU-days on A100/H100 class | **Do this second. This is the real answer to "her own model".** |
| **3. Continued pre-training** | Another 10-100B tokens of finance corpus on top of open weights. | Weeks of multi-node cluster time, six figures of compute if rented | Possible in principle, out of scope in practice. Revisit only with a supervisor and an allocation. |
| **4. Foundation model from scratch** | What "an entirely new model" literally means. | ~30M GPU-hours, trillions of curated tokens, a team of dozens, $50-100M | **Not achievable.** Not on any university cluster, not by a solo developer. Say this plainly and early. |

**Tier 2 is the goal.** A 70B base, fine-tuned on this system's own accumulated
outcomes and on the owner's own writing, is a model that genuinely does not
exist anywhere else and is genuinely hers. That is not a consolation prize for
tier 4 — it is what every serious applied-AI team actually does, and it is
defensible in front of anyone who knows the field.

### Establish these before writing code

You cannot size any of this against "a powerful university computer". Find out,
in this order:

1. **The actual hardware.** `nvidia-smi`, `sinfo`, `scontrol show partition`.
   How many GPUs, which model, how much VRAM each, how many nodes.
2. **How jobs run.** Almost certainly SLURM with a wall-clock limit — often
   12-48h. **A batch cluster cannot host a 24/7 service.** This is the single
   biggest architectural constraint and it is easy to miss until the first job
   is killed mid-conversation.
3. **The acceptable-use policy.** University compute is provisioned for
   research and teaching. A personal trading system running continuously may
   breach it, and the consequence is a suspended account, not a warning. **The
   owner must check this with the cluster administrators before anything is
   deployed.** Raise it explicitly; do not let it be discovered later.
4. **Data governance.** The Obsidian vault contains the owner's personal notes
   and the prediction log will contain other people's sign-in data. Putting
   either on university storage may be a data-protection issue. Default to
   keeping personal data off the cluster entirely — fine-tune on de-identified
   exports, not on raw vault contents.

If the cluster turns out to be batch-only — and it probably is — the honest
architecture is: **train and fine-tune on the cluster, serve from elsewhere.**
The laptop's RTX 4060 (8 GB) can serve a 7-8B distilled or quantised version of
whatever is fine-tuned. That is a real, working design and it should be planned
for from the start rather than reached in defeat.

## THE BRIEF

### 1. Serve the large model (tier 1)

Behind the existing `src/inference/` router — this is a provider change, not a
rewrite. vLLM for throughput, llama.cpp if VRAM is tight. Keep the router
interface; six subsystems already speak to it and none should need editing.

Bound the context deliberately and truncate by a stated rule. The vault
injection and debate transcripts will overflow a naive setup silently.

### 2. Make it hers (tier 2) — the part that matters

**The training data already exists in this repository, and it is the point.**

`src/v5/track_record.py:training_export()` emits every resolved prediction as a
supervised row: the 41-module feature vector in, the realised outcome out. That
is a dataset nobody else has, generated by this system's own operation.

Build three corpora:

- **Outcomes** — `training_export()`. Teaches her what her own signals were
  worth. This is the highest-value data in the project.
- **Voice** — the owner's Obsidian vault, de-identified. Teaches her how he
  thinks and writes.
- **Domain** — filings, papers, textbooks via the ingestion pipeline, with
  sources retained so citations stay real.

Then QLoRA the 70B on it. Evaluate held-out, chronologically split, never
shuffled — the same discipline `src/v5/weightfit.py` already enforces for the
ensemble weights, and for the same reason.

**The dependency to state out loud: there are currently ZERO resolved
predictions.** `training_export()` returns an empty list today. The outcome
corpus does not exist yet and cannot be manufactured — it accrues at the rate
the paper loop resolves calls, roughly twenty directional calls over about a
month. Fine-tuning on voice and domain can start now; fine-tuning on outcomes
waits for reality. Do not let anyone paper over this by generating synthetic
outcomes. A model fine-tuned on invented results is worse than no fine-tune,
because it will be confidently wrong in exactly the domain that matters.

### 3. Evolving

"Evolves over time" means a scheduled loop that is honest about what changed:

- Periodic re-fine-tune as resolved calls accumulate, versioned, with the
  previous adapter kept so a regression can be rolled back.
- Every new adapter is evaluated against the incumbent on a held-out set
  BEFORE it is promoted. If it does not win, it is not adopted. `weightfit.py`
  already implements exactly this logic for ensemble weights — read it and copy
  the shape.
- Adopting a worse model because it is newer is the failure mode. Guard it.

### 4. Senses

- **Read** — the ingestion pipeline into the vault index. Cite or stay silent.
- **See** — a multimodal model (Qwen2-VL class) for charts and screenshots.
  A separate concern from market data, which she already "sees" numerically.
- **Hear / speak** — Whisper locally for STT; `frontend/src/core/ariaVoice.js`
  already exists for TTS.
- **Create** — writing, analysis, code. Falls out of tiers 1-2.

### 5. "Feel" and "sense" — build the useful version

There is a real capability here and a fake one, and they are easy to confuse.

**Real, and worth building:** affective *perception* — reading sentiment and
urgency in the owner's messages and in market text, and modulating behaviour
(when to interrupt, when to stay quiet, how much hedging a briefing needs).
Also *interoception* in the engineering sense: knowing her own state — stale
data, an unreachable brain, a stalled loop — and saying so unprompted.

**Fake, and forbidden:** claiming to have emotions she does not have. Do not
write "I'm excited about this trade" or a mood indicator driven by a random
seed. She has no feelings, and simulating them in a system that gives financial
information is a way of borrowing trust she has not earned.

The distinction to hold: she can *recognise* feeling and *report her own
state*. She does not *have* feelings. Write her to be frank about that if asked
— it is a better answer than a performance.

### 6. Self-modification and the protected set

Unchanged from `docs/ARIA_OWN_BRAIN_PROMPT.md` §2, and it becomes MORE
important as she becomes more capable, not less. Build
`tests/test_protected_paths.py` first. Propose → test → human merge. Protected:
obey the creator; live-money auto-trade stays off; the auth layer; and the list
itself.

A more capable model does not get more latitude with the fence. It is the
reason she can be given latitude everywhere else.

## THE STANDARD THIS PROJECT IS HELD TO

Four adversarial review rounds found three separate overstatements in this
system's own measurement code — each one flattering, each one caught and
corrected and written down. That record is the most valuable thing here, more
than any model.

A bigger model makes ARIA more capable. It does not make her more trustworthy,
and the temptation as capability grows is to let the prose outrun the evidence.
If she cannot cite it, she does not claim it. If the sample is thin, she says
the sample is thin. If she does not know, she says so.

Build her a superb mind. Do not let her become a confident one.
